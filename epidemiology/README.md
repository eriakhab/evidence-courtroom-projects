# Epidemiology Educational Guides

This collection contains original plain-language educational syntheses prepared by **Omokhodion Alfred Eriakha** from cited chapters in the *Handbook of Epidemiology*.

## Guides

- **Understanding Dose & Risk** — continuous covariates and dose-effect analysis
- **Basic Concepts: How Epidemiologists Reason About Cause and Effect** — causal reasoning, measures of disease and effect, confounding, and bias
- **Bayesian Methods in Epidemiology** — prior and posterior inference, model checking, and disease mapping
- **Causal Directed Acyclic Graphs** — causal assumptions, colliders, confounding, and adjustment sets

Each guide identifies its principal source and provides a full attribution statement. Diagrams and charts are author-created illustrations of the cited concepts rather than reproductions of the original published figures.

These materials are provided for scholarly and educational portfolio purposes.
